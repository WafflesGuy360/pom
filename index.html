PROJECT OMEGA

the papers are waiting

BACKSTORY

You are already asleep. You just don’t know it yet.

This place is not a building. It is a dream that refuses to end — a labyrinth of red brick and dead lights, folded in on itself so tightly that the waking world has become a rumor. Ten pages were torn from something older than language and scattered through the corridors. Collect them all, and the dream may finally release you.

Fail, and the five minutes run out. Then He arrives. You will never see Him. You will only feel the walls begin to breathe, the writing start to move, and a sound that grows until there is nothing left of you but the echo of your own name.

Those who stay too long do not wake up. The dream keeps them. Forever.

HOW TO PLAY

WASD — Move through the maze

Shift — Run

Mouse or ← → — Look around

Find and collect all 10 papers before the timer reaches zero.

A green arrow at the top of the screen points toward the nearest uncollected paper.

If the timer ends before you gather every page, something arrives. You cannot fight it. You can only run — and running will not be enough.

DOSSIER

Designation: BASEMENT — also logged as Level 0.

The structure does not appear on any facility map. Staff who report a “green room” and “endless carpeted halls” are usually written off as exhausted. They are not exhausted. They have crossed into a closed loop of yellowed wallpaper, dead fluorescent pools, and doors that lead only to more of the same.

Inside, papers keep appearing on the floor. No one knows who prints them. Collect them and carry them to the table in the green safe room. That room is the only cell the entity will not enter. Turn the pages in there and the system pays you in silence and a little more time.

The entity is called Take Care. It does not run until it sees you. It does not speak. It learns the last place it had line of sight and walks there with the patience of something that has already outlived every witness. Static climbs the walls when it is near. The lights dip. Your vision shreds. If it touches you outside the green room, you drop every paper you were carrying and the loop resets around your body.

There is no timer in the Basement. There is no exit condition published in the logs. The instruction left on the first recovered tape is four words long: survive, collect, turn in. The second tape adds one more: it cannot enter green.

VHS overlay optional. Footsteps optional. Looking away is not. The halls do not empty when you close your eyes. Take Care is already walking them. The papers are already waiting. The green room is the only kindness the place has ever shown, and even that may be a trap that simply has not finished closing.

Enter Basement only if you are willing to be lost for a while. The dream does not guarantee you will be found again.
